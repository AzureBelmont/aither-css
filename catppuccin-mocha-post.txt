A custom CSS theme built on top of Aither Dark — a port of the much-loved [url=https://github.com/catppuccin/catppuccin]Catppuccin[/url] palette (Mocha flavour) to Aither. Soothing pastel surfaces, a deep eye-friendly layered background, and clean panel styling throughout the site.

Ships in [b]six accent flavours[/b] — Mauve, Peach, Blue, Red, Green, and Maroon — so you can pick the one you like. Same theme, same layout, only the accent changes. Install whichever URL you prefer (see [b]Install[/b] below).

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Catppuccin Mocha palette with a three-layer depth system — [i]crust[/i] page background, [i]mantle[/i] nav/headers/cards, [i]base[/i] content areas
[*]Six accent options — Mauve, Peach, Blue, Red, Green, Maroon
[*]Accent-coloured panel headings, active nav underline, and notification dots
[*]Accent left-border on hovered torrent rows and unread forum topics
[*]Accent-tinted logo with hover glow (colour-matched per flavour)
[*]Fully styled chatbox, dropdowns, inputs, and SweetAlert toasts
[*]Slim accent scrollbar
[*]Two-tone striping (base over mantle) on stats and key-value rows
[*]One-line group requirement cards — name, perk icons, and expand button on a single row
[*]Consistent advanced-search filters — inputs, selects, region/distributor dropdowns, and checkbox boxes all share one surface
[*]All rank colours preserved (UNIT3D originals)
[/list]

[b]Install[/b]
Go to Settings → General, set your theme to [b]Aither Dark[/b], then paste [b]one[/b] of the following into [b]External CSS Stylesheet (Stacks on top of above theme)[/b] depending on which accent you want:

[b]Mauve[/b]
[code]https://azurebelmont.github.io/aither-css/catppuccin-mocha.css[/code]
[b]Peach[/b]
[code]https://azurebelmont.github.io/aither-css/catppuccin-mocha-peach.css[/code]
[b]Blue[/b]
[code]https://azurebelmont.github.io/aither-css/catppuccin-mocha-blue.css[/code]
[b]Red[/b]
[code]https://azurebelmont.github.io/aither-css/catppuccin-mocha-red.css[/code]
[b]Green[/b]
[code]https://azurebelmont.github.io/aither-css/catppuccin-mocha-green.css[/code]
[b]Maroon[/b]
[code]https://azurebelmont.github.io/aither-css/catppuccin-mocha-maroon.css[/code]
